Complete the case to receive scored decisions and explanatory feedback. Results and action plans persist in the on-device case history. Four cases are included. This is implemented in native Flutter widgets, not a website or webview.

After signing in with the existing review account, Home now leads with "Öğrenme görevine devam et". Paths contain native multiple-choice, drag/drop, information cards, written answers, audio playback and optional microphone recordings, with server-validated XP and progress. Completed paths can be restarted for practice. Review credentials remain in the private sign-in fields.
